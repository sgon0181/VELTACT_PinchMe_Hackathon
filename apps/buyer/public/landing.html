From line stop to committed specialist, in one workflow.

Veltact takes an industrial problem from evidence to an actionable, commercially committed response.

01 / Find

Make the need decision-ready.

Veltact structures the evidence and returns current, cited solution pathways, including what the factory can prepare and when specialist help is required.

Output: selected solution scope
02 / Connect

Turn relevance into response.

RapidMatch turns the chosen scope into one standard request and contacts the most relevant suppliers in parallel. Suppliers return comparable commercial intent, not search results.

Output: selected supplier response
03 / Deploy

Secure attention, then track delivery.

Pinch turns the selected response into a commitment for the site assessment. Backend payment evidence and delivery progress remain distinct and visible.

Output: secured engagement
