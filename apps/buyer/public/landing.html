01 / Workflow

01 One requirement, no broken handoffs

Find the path. Connect the response. Deploy with control.

Veltact keeps industrial evidence, buyer decisions, supplier intent and commitment state attached to the same requirement.

  1. 01 / Find

    Make the need decision-ready.

    Written context, reports and photos become a buyer-reviewed Need Profile with cited pathways, missing information and safe preparation steps.

    Output: selected solution scope
  2. 02 / Connect

    Turn relevance into response.

    Explainable matches receive buyer-approved outreach. Suppliers return availability, indicative price, experience, approach and conditions in one format.

    Output: selected supplier response
  3. 03 / Deploy

    Secure attention, then track delivery.

    A hosted Pinch checkout funds the agreed commitment. Backend payment evidence and delivery progress remain distinct and visible.

    Output: secured engagement
